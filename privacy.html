Nikto Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026 · Nikto is made by ognoTongo

Short version: your todos stay on your phone. Nikto has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics. We never see your data.

What Nikto stores

Nikto stores the todos you create (titles, notes, due dates, times, priority, tags, repeat rules and completion history) and your settings (reminder times and on/off switches). All of it is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. Nikto does not send it to ognoTongo or anyone else.

What Nikto doesn't collect

  • No account or sign-in, and no name, email address or phone number.
  • No location, contacts, photos or files.
  • No advertising or advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs.

Features that involve Google

A few optional features use services built into Android. They're provided by Google under Google's Privacy Policy, not ours.

  • Voice input. When you tap a mic button, Nikto asks your phone's speech recognizer (normally Google's) to turn what you say into text. The recognizer handles the audio; Nikto only receives the resulting text and never gets microphone access itself.
  • Gemini and other assistants. If you ask Gemini to use Nikto (for example, "add clean room today in Nikto"), Gemini calls Nikto's functions on your phone to add, list, complete or reschedule todos. The information those functions return, such as todo titles and due dates, goes to Gemini so it can answer you, and Google handles it under its own terms. Nikto only does this when an assistant asks on your behalf, and only Android-authorized assistants can ask.
  • Device backup. If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include Nikto's data in your device backup (for example, to your Google account), so it can be restored on a new phone. You control this in your phone's backup settings.

Sharing into Nikto

If you share text or a link from another app to Nikto, it is saved as a todo on your device and nowhere else.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for the morning brief and evening check-in.
  • Alarms & reminders: so reminders arrive on time.
  • Run at startup: to set reminders again after your phone restarts.

Keeping or deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. Deleting a todo in the app removes it from your list. Uninstalling Nikto, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes everything Nikto has stored. We hold no copy, so there's nothing for us to delete or hand over.

Children

Nikto isn't directed to children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

A future version may add optional sign-in and cloud sync, so your todos can be shared with family members or reached on the web. That would mean storing some data on our servers. Before any such feature ships, we'll update this policy to explain exactly what is stored, where and for how long, and we'll change the effective date above.
